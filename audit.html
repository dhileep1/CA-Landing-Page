Audit & Assurance Practice

Rigorous, independent auditing services providing stakeholders, financial institutions, and regulators with complete financial transparency.

1. Statutory Audit under Companies Act 2013

Mandatory independent audit of financial statements for Private Limited Companies, Public Limited Companies, and LLPs ensuring compliance with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS / AS).

2. Tax Audit under Section 44AB

Detailed examination of business books of account for businesses with turnover exceeding ₹1 Crore (or ₹10 Crores for digital transactions) and professionals u/s 44ADA.

3. Internal Audit & Risk Advisory

Evaluating internal financial controls, operational efficiency, fraud prevention, and process optimization to protect enterprise value.
